# Agamon: Forever

Agamon: Forever is an addon for World of Warcraft: Forever, client build 70205 and interface 16001. It displays spell damage and healing estimates, resource efficiency, gear valuation, rotation estimates, auction comparisons, and combat-scenario simulations.

## Manual Installation

To manually install, copy the `AgamonForever` folder into the game's `Interface/AddOns` directory.

## Features

- Damage and healing estimates for spells, attacks, and damage-over-time effects.
- Resource efficiency and combo-point finisher comparisons.
- Damage and survivability estimates for buffs, debuffs, stats, weapons, and gear.
- Rotation estimates with modeled openers and ability sequences.
- Scenario comparisons for damage, healing, tanking, and survival against bosses or groups.
- Gear upgrades, best-in-slot rankings, crafting costs, and vendor-price comparisons that learn as you play.
- BIS, upgrade, vendor, and craft rows are saved per character and reused until their inputs change; use the list-load toggle to choose the frame-friendly default or a faster scan.
- Auction House price tracking to help identify upgrades, valuable crafts to make based on reagent costs, and vendor deals.
- Optional mana bar for Cat, Bear, and Dire Bear Forms.
- Experimental rotation simulation for optimizing dps, healing, and survivability across different scenarios

## Commands

Use `/agf help` in game for the complete command list and descriptions. Common commands include `/agf options`, `/agf items`, `/agf bis`, `/agf vendor`, `/agf craft`, `/agf rotation`, `/agf simulate`, `/agf mana`, and `/agf forget`.

## Model limitations

Simulations are EXPERIMENTAL and not fully accurate to the game, not all mechanics, procs, or stats are implemented.  

Take them with a grain of salt.
